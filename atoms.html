What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on the user's equipment or mobile device (henceforth collectively referred to as "devices"), via a browser. The placement of cookies allows the website to recognize your device on a next visit. When we use the term cookies in this policy we refer to all files that collect information in this way. Depending on the type of cookies, the refusal to install them may result in the impossibility of accessing some of their areas or receiving personalized information. At any time, through your browser, you can decide to be notified of the receipt of cookies, as well as block their installation on your devices.
