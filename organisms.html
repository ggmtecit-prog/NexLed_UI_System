Rollout Notes

Review the regional migration checklist

Each region needs the latest catalog mapping, warehouse staffing confirmation, and pricing export before the migration starts.

Once the rollout begins, all new store and repair orders will use the updated availability service, while existing draft orders keep the previous rules until they are resubmitted.

Teams should also verify that language packs are downloaded on support kiosks, barcode scanners have synced overnight, and return labels are pointed at the new logistics provider.

If a region cannot complete those checks in time, pause the activation for that site and keep the migration blocked until the final validation report is approved.
